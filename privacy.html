Privacy Policy — Crazy Stack: Clay Builders

Last updated: October 27, 2026

Crazy Stack: Clay Builders ("the app", "we") is a game for iPhone and iPad. This policy explains what information the app handles.

We do not collect your personal information

  • The app does not require an account or login.
  • The app does not send any data to us or to third parties. It works fully offline.
  • The app contains no advertising, no analytics or tracking software, and no in-app purchases.
  • We do not track you across other companies' apps or websites.

Data stored on your device

Your game progress, best times, settings (such as language and sound), outfits, Stone Book entries, achievements and play statistics are saved on your device only. Deleting the app removes them.

Game Center

If you are signed in to Apple Game Center, your scores and achievements are sent to Apple's Game Center service so they can appear on leaderboards. This is handled by Apple under Apple's privacy policy; we do not receive your Game Center data. You can play without Game Center.

Sharing

When you tap "Share", the app opens the standard iOS share sheet with an image from your game and a short text. You choose whether and where to send it. We do not receive it. The app does not use the camera, microphone, location or contacts.

Children

The app does not knowingly collect any information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the date above.
