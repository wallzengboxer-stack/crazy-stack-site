Support — Crazy Stack: Clay Builders

Questions, bugs or ideas? Email wallzengboxer@gmail.com — please include your device model and iOS version.

Quick help

  • Drag to drive the stone, flick sideways to fling / ram / toss it, flick up to flip it, flick down to stomp and bounce to the next stone.
  • Tap a cell of the stone you're riding to chop it off — the chip goes to the porter's basket and he plugs holes with it.
  • Tap a landed stone to dig it out (open a covered hole).
  • Reach the bunting with no holes to win. Touch the storm clouds and the wall falls.
  • Co-op: turn on Co-op in the menu. Player 1 uses the top half of the screen, player 2 the bottom half.
  • Language: Settings & Help → Language (14 languages).
  • Game Center: sign in under iOS Settings → Game Center to see leaderboards and achievements.
